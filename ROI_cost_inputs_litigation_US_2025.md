# ROI inputs for U.S. litigation firms (2025)

**Scope.** Costs reflect typical U.S. law‑firm workflows that still involve creating/handling physical binders for litigation (briefs/exhibits). Where ranges exist, I provide a *best default* and the defensible range. All sources are dated or evergreen and linked inline; short derivations are shown below.

**The calculator this feeds:** <https://sam-mfb.github.io/roi/>

## Evidence & derivations

### 1) Production costs

- **Black‑and‑white printing (laser)**
  - 5–8¢/page typical; default **$0.06**/page.  
    Sources: [TonerBuzz](https://www.tonerbuzz.com/blog/printing-costs/); [RTINGS](https://www.rtings.com/printer/tests/printing/cost-per-print).
  - **Alternative derivations:** Use cost‑per‑page calculator at [ctrlcalculator.com](https://ctrlcalculator.com/financial/print-cost-per-page-calculator/) or managed print contract CPP.

- **Binder materials**
  - Walmart “Office Essentials” 3″ binders (4‑pack $18.99) ⇒ $4.75/binder.  
    [Walmart link](https://www.walmart.com/browse/walmart-for-business/3-inch-binders/6735581_3020544_5093993_3859294_8518720)
  - Staples exhibit tabs $9.99/set.  
    [Staples tabs](https://www.staples.com/buy/legal-exhibit-dividers-0alz03a)
  - Heavy‑duty 3″ binders $20.52/binder.  
    [Staples heavy‑duty binders](https://www.staples.com/staples-heavy-duty-3-3-ring-view-binders-d-ring-black-4-pack-56234ct-24690ct/product_2723723)
  - **Default**: $4.75 + $9.99 = $14.74 per binder.

### 2) Labor rates

- **Paralegal loaded hourly cost**  
  - Wage: $61,010 / 2,080 = $29.33/hr (BLS OOH).  
    [BLS OOH](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/Legal/Paralegals-and-legal-assistants.htm)  
  - Benefits factor: 70.3% wage share (ECEC) ⇒ 1.422× = $41.72/hr.  
    [BLS ECEC](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ecec_09102024.pdf)
- **Attorney rates (optional time savings)**  
  - [Clio Legal Trends Report](https://www.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/)  
  - [Thomson Reuters State of the U.S. Legal Market 2025](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/posts/wp-content/uploads/sites/20/2025/01/State-of-the-US-Legal-Market-Report-2025.pdf)

### 3) Logistics & storage

- **Shipping per binder**  
  - FedEx Express Saver: Small $16.75, Medium $21.00.  
    [FedEx One Rate PDF](https://www.fedex.com/content/dam/fedex/us-united-states/services/OneRate-Pricing_2025.pdf)
- **Storage per binder per year**  
  - Vendor range $0.50–$0.95/box/month (~4 binders/box) ⇒ $1.50–$2.85/binder/year.  
    [Record Nations](https://www.recordnations.com/resources/cost-document-storage/)  
    [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_us/insights/forensic-integrity-services/reduce-operating-costs-by-limiting-off-site-storage-use)  
    [UVA Records Mgmt](https://recordsmanagement.virginia.edu/services-pricing)  
  - **Default:** $2.00 per binder/year.

## Alternative derivations

- **Printing:** Managed print CPP (0.8–1.5¢/page) from [Northern AZ IT](https://www.northernazit.com/blog/how-much-does-managed-print-services-cost/).
- **Labor:** Use BLS OEWS state data (May 2024) × 1.422 load factor.  
  [BLS OEWS state lookup](https://www.bls.gov/oes/current/oessrcst.htm)
- **Shipping:** UPS Simple Rate small business pricing.  
  [UPS small-business rates](https://www.ups.com/us/en/shipping/zones-and-rates/small-business-rates)
- **Storage (onsite):** rent $/sqft × 0.25 sqft/binder footprint × utilization.
